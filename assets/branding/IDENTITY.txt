Design & Engineering Philosophy
- Control: user-owned actions, explicit invocation, and native shell behavior preserved.
- Precision: readable state, clear signal, and context-aware presentation without noise.
- Security-minded pragmatism: local heuristics and transparent limitations over hype.
- Recovery & trust: backups, rollback paths, and verification over blind automation.
- Craft: cinematic visual identity and disciplined engineering can coexist.

Founder / Creator Statement
HN NEXUS is engineered as a refined command environment that balances aesthetic presence with operational clarity. It is built to help operators stay oriented, work deliberately, and recover safely.
